# Privacy Policy

**App name:** Rishta: Marriage Biodata Maker (Android package `app.rishta.biodata`)
**Developer:** CodeOrbit Labs
**Contact:** er.varunvision@gmail.com
**Last updated:** November 20, 2026


This policy covers the Android app "Rishta: Marriage Biodata Maker". In short: what you type and your photos stay on your phone. The app has no accounts. It uses Google services for ads, purchases, usage statistics and notifications, and this page says exactly what those receive.

## What stays on your phone

- Everything you type into a biodata or a card: names, dates, family details, religion, community, horoscope and kundli details, contact numbers and addresses.
- Your photos, and the result of removing a photo's background.
- Your saved documents. They are stored encrypted, with a key kept in Android's secure key store.
- Backup files. A backup is encrypted with a password you choose, and it goes only where you save or send it. We never see it or the password.
- App lock. It uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or screen lock. The app never sees your fingerprint or face.

None of this is sent to us, to Google, to advertisers or to anyone else. It leaves your phone only when you export or share a file yourself, and then only to the app or place you choose.

## What the app sends, and to whom

**No server of ours in this version.** Every design is inside the app. This version does not download anything from us and does not send us anything, not even anonymous counts. If a later version downloads designs or counts popular designs, this policy will say so first.

**Usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics), only when switched on.** You choose this under Settings → Privacy choices. Where the law asks for consent first (for example in the European Union and the United Kingdom), it starts switched off. When on, the app records a fixed list of events, such as which screen was opened, which design was viewed, that an export finished, and that a design was unlocked. An event may carry the design's id, the document type (biodata, card or video) and the document's language, and nothing else from the app. Firebase also collects an app-instance id, your device model and Android version, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. Google processes this for us under its own terms.

**Ads (Google AdMob).** Free use of the app is paid for by ads. Ads appear only on Home, the design galleries and My files: a small banner, an ad card among the designs, and now and then a full-screen ad when you move between those screens, finish an export or come back to the app after some hours. Full-screen ads are at least three minutes apart, and you can also choose to watch one to unlock designs. Ads never appear on the screens where you type or over a preview of your document, and not at all once you buy Lifetime, Remove ads or a 7-day pass. To show ads, Google receives your phone's advertising id, IP address, device information and how you interact with the ads. Ads never receive anything you typed. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad is requested, and you can change it later under Settings → Privacy choices. You can also turn off personalised ads there, and reset or delete your advertising id in Android's settings.

**Notifications (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging).** If you allow notifications, we can send news about new designs to everyone using the same app or language. Firebase uses a token to deliver them. We cannot send a message to you individually.

**Purchases (Google Play).** Payment for Rishta Lifetime, Remove ads and the 7-day pass is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Each time it starts, the app asks Google Play on your phone what you own; a 7-day pass runs from the time Google Play records the payment, and it does not renew.

**Background removal (Google Play services).** Removing a photo's background happens on your phone. The first time, Google Play services may download the part that does this, and it may send Google anonymous statistics about how the feature performs. Your photo is never sent.

## What we do not do

- We do not have accounts, and we do not know who you are.
- We do not sell personal information.
- We do not ask for location, contacts, camera roll access, or any other permission to read your data. When you add a photo, Android lets you pick just that one.
- We never use religion, caste or community for ads or statistics; that information never leaves your phone.

## Your choices

- Settings → Privacy choices: usage statistics, personalised ads, and (where it applies) Google's ad consent form.
- Settings → Delete all my data: removes every biodata, card, photo and setting from the app.
- Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone.
- Android settings: reset or delete your advertising id; turn off notifications.

Because we do not hold your documents or know who you are, there is nothing on our side to request, correct or delete. Data that Google processes for analytics and ads is kept according to Google's policies.

## Children

The app is meant for adults planning a marriage. It is not directed at children, and its Google Play audience is set to 18 and over.

## Security

Documents are encrypted on the phone. This version downloads nothing from us. If a later version downloads designs or settings, each is checked against a digital signature before it is used, so a changed file is refused, and every connection uses HTTPS.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on the store listing, with a new date at the top.
